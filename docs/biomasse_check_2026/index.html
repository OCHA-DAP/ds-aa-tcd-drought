⚠️ NOT OFFICIAL MONITORING — EXPLORATORY MID-SEASON CHECK

The Window 3 trigger of the Chad drought AA framework is evaluated once, in early September, on end-of-season biomasse (dekad 24, 21–31 August). This page applies the same methodology to the latest available dekad — dekad 21 (21–31 July 2026) — purely as an early, informal look at the season so far. The season is not finished, the 84.5 % threshold was calibrated for dekad 24 (not dekad 21), and a substitute data layer was used (see caveats). This page has no operational standing and must not be used for activation decisions.

How to read this — caveats

  • The season can still recover. Dekad-21 and dekad-24 anomalies correlate historically (r ≈ 0.84), but in 2002 and 2013 the AOI was below 84.5 % at dekad 21 and finished above it at dekad 24. August rainfall decides. That said, both recovery years were borderline at dekad 21 (74 % and 84 %); the only year lower than 2026 at this dekad (2015, 51.6 %) went on to trigger.
  • Substitute data source. The dekadal feed used by the repo pipeline (WA_DMP_ADM1_ef_v1) has not updated since dekad 31 of 2025, so this check uses the dekadal DMP columns of GeoSahel's WA_BIO_ADM1_v4 layer (observed data through dekad 21 of 2026, no climatological fill). Validation: at dekad 24 that source reproduces the framework's five backtest activation years (2006, 2009, 2011, 2015, 2017), but also puts 2014 marginally below threshold (84.2 %), and year-level anomalies differ from the operational feed by a few percentage points — treat all values here as approximate.
  • The threshold does not formally apply at dekad 21. 84.5 % was calibrated on dekad-24 values for a ~5-year return period. At dekad 21 the same cutoff would have flagged 8 of 27 historical years — a more frequent event.
  • Consistent with the forecast windows. Windows 1 and 2 already triggered on 30 April and 9 May 2026 on SEAS5 forecasts of a poor July–September season; this observational reading points the same way.

What actually matters: the official Window 3 check runs in early September on dekad 24 (21–31 August) data, verified with DREM before any notification.
